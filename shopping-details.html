Product Information

This hooded thermal anorak is crafted for cold-weather comfort without sacrificing style. It features a quilted inner lining that traps heat efficiently, an adjustable hood for added protection against wind and rain, and long sleeves with adjustable cuff tabs for a customizable fit. The asymmetric hem with elastic side tabs keeps the jacket secure during movement, while the front zip fastening with placket adds a clean, structured finish. Ideal for layering in autumn and winter.

Material Used

Made from a premium cotton-polyester blend, this anorak balances durability with breathability. Unlike low-grade polyester — which creases easily, lacks breathability, and often develops an unwanted shine that cheapens the overall look — this fabric is treated for a matte, natural finish that holds its shape wash after wash. The quilted lining uses a soft brushed interior for extra warmth without adding bulk, making it comfortable enough for all-day wear.
