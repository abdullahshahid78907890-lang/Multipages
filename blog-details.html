5 Grooming Habits Every Man Should Build Into His Routine

By Abdullah Shahid | August 27, 2026 | 8 Comments

SHARE

A good skincare routine isn't just for women anymore — men are catching up, and for good reason. Daily exposure to sun, pollution, and stress takes a visible toll on the skin over time, showing up as dullness, fine lines, and uneven texture.

Start with the basics: a gentle cleanser to remove dirt and excess oil, followed by a lightweight moisturizer that keeps skin hydrated without feeling greasy. Sunscreen is non-negotiable — even on cloudy days — since UV exposure is one of the leading causes of premature aging.

Consistency matters more than complexity. Sticking to a simple routine every day will always outperform an elaborate one used only occasionally.

"Good skin isn't about using more products it's about using the right ones, consistently."

_ ABDULLAH SHAHID _

Layering fabrics the right way keeps you comfortable without sacrificing style. A well-chosen base layer, paired with a structured outer piece, works for both cold mornings and warmer afternoons.

Investing in a few quality pieces always pays off more than filling your closet with fast fashion. Fabrics that last, fits that flatter — that's what makes an outfit feel effortless.

Abdullah Shahid
